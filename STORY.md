# Snowcrash — Original Story Arc

This document summarizes **our** game’s original courier story. Themes echo cyberpunk Metaverse vibes at a high level only — no novel text, no borrowed character names.

## Premise

You are **Rin Vale**, freelance Metaverse courier/hacker working the fractured franchise streets of LA. Meatspace is a patchwork of courier alleys and branded enclaves; the Metaverse is a parallel social layer humming above the asphalt.

Someone dumped **Payload-Zero** — neurolinguistic malware shaped like a speech-act weapon — into an old street jackpoint. Infected avatars glitch hard. Security drones triangulate. Your job: sleeve the core, reach a Metaverse uplink, and scrub it with a counter-incantation (a Babel fracture / uplink scrub) before it rewrites the Layer.

## Story beats & cutscenes

1. **Intro chapters** — fractured cities, Metaverse overlay, Payload-Zero threat, Cable Baron rumor, jack-in.
2. **`briefing_librarian`** — after talking to Relay Tran (or Archive Daemon): dossier on Payload-Zero and the uplink scrub.
3. **`club_black_neon`** — enter Club Glassline / talk DJ: street weapons, infected avatars, loud cover for quiet deals.
4. **`flotilla_signal`** — radio/terminal find or mid-quest flag: Cassian Vox’s Flotilla bleeds propaganda and refugees across the Layer.
5. **`payload` / jackpoint** — acquire Faraday-sleeved Payload-Zero (personal clone in MMORPG).
6. **`namshub_counter`** (+ uplink) — at delivery, counter-incantation fractures the linguistic payload into harmless checksums.
7. **`street_victory` / `babel_clear`** — personal quest win: streets breathe; Babel quiet returns.

## Player agency note

Wishes (`/wish`, `/feature`) let couriers petition the street layer. Matched petitions can mint prototype toys; unmatched ones log a Backlog Token. Flavor only — not a novel mechanic transplant.
